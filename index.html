01 Who we are

A flexible remote team built to support growing businesses

We are a collaborative remote team with different areas of expertise across sales support, lead generation, cold calling, social media, graphic design, video editing, data analysis, data entry, presentation design, and basic GHL support.

Each team member has a primary specialty, but we work collaboratively and support one another when workloads change or additional assistance is needed.

Our goal is to give businesses access to a reliable team that can handle multiple day-to-day tasks without the need to hire and manage several separate freelancers.

11 Our workflow

One team. One organized process.

01

Client request

The client provides the task, objective, instructions, and deadline.

02

Team coordination

The team lead reviews the request and determines who is best suited for the task.

03

Task assignment

The task is assigned to the appropriate team member.

04

Work in progress

The assigned member completes the task based on the client's requirements.

05

Quality check

The work is reviewed when necessary before delivery.

06

Client delivery

The completed work is submitted with a clear update.

07

Follow-up

Feedback, revisions, and upcoming priorities are tracked by the team.

Team collaboration

Although every member has a primary specialty, we believe in cross-support. When a member has available capacity, they can assist another member so tasks continue moving and deadlines are met.
